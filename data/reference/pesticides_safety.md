# Pesticides: classes and safe use (EDUCATIONAL GUIDANCE)

> Only use products **registered by the Pest Control Products Board (PCPB)** for that crop and pest. Always follow the label for dose,
> pre-harvest interval and re-entry time. This page gives no doses on purpose.

| Target | Option class | Examples of active ingredients registered in Kenya (check current label) |
|---|---|---|
| Fall Armyworm | Biological | *Bacillus thuringiensis* (Bt), *Metarhizium anisopliae* |
| Fall Armyworm | Chemical | emamectin benzoate, chlorantraniliprole, spinetoram |
| Late Blight | Protectant fungicide | mancozeb, copper hydroxide |
| Late Blight | Systemic + protectant | metalaxyl + mancozeb, cymoxanil + mancozeb |
| Aphids | Low-toxicity | insecticidal soap, neem (azadirachtin) |
| Aphids | Chemical | acetamiprid, lambda-cyhalothrin (harms beneficial insects) |

## Safe use checklist
1. **Is spraying needed?** Scout first; spray only above the action threshold.
2. **Check the weather.** Do not spray if rain is likely within 6–24 h (spray-safety index UNSAFE) or in wind.
3. **Protect yourself:** gloves, mask, long sleeves, boots; never eat, drink or smoke while spraying.
4. **Protect others:** keep children and animals away; never spray near rivers, wells or beehives in the day.
5. **Respect the pre-harvest interval (PHI)** on the label before harvesting or selling.
6. **Rotate modes of action** (IRAC/FRAC groups) to slow resistance.
7. **Empty containers:** triple-rinse, puncture, and return to a collection point. Never reuse them for water or food.

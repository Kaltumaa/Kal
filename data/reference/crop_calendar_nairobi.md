# Crop calendar — Nairobi highlands, ~1,700 m (SYNTHETIC GUIDANCE)

Short rains: onset usually mid-October to early November. Long rains: March to May.
Planting rule of thumb used by this desk: plant once ≥ 25 mm of rain is expected over 7 days and no storm / washout warning is active.

| Crop | Stages (days after planting) |
|---|---|
| Maize | 0–10 emergence · 11–45 early vegetative · 46–75 late vegetative/tasseling · 76–110 grain fill · 111–150 maturity |
| Beans | 0–10 emergence · 11–35 vegetative · 36–55 flowering · 56–85 pod fill · 86+ maturity |
| Kale | 0–21 establishment · 22+ continuous harvest |
| Potato | 0–20 emergence · 21–50 vegetative · 51–75 tuber initiation · 76–110 bulking · 111+ maturity |
| Tomato | 0–25 establishment · 26–55 vegetative/flowering · 56–100 fruiting · 101+ harvest |

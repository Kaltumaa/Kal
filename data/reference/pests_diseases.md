# Pests & diseases of the Nairobi highlands (desk guidance, synthetic)

## Officer targets
- **Fall Armyworm:** scout 20 plants in a W; act at ≥ 10 % fresh damage (≥ 20 % after knee height), from emergence to tasseling.
- **Late Blight:** in cool (12–20 °C), wet weeks, advise a protectant before rain and removing infected plants the same day.
- **Aphids:** act only when colonies spread beyond a few plants; soap or neem first.
- **Report it:** confirm any outbreak with a field visit or *Field scan* so it becomes a verified alert the agent can use.

> Written for this project from general public extension practice. Confirm with your extension officer.

## Fall Armyworm (*Spodoptera frugiperda*): maize, sorghum
- **Signs:** "window-pane" scraping on young leaves, ragged holes, sawdust-like frass in the whorl, larvae with an inverted "Y" on the head.
- **Risk window:** emergence to tasseling (days 0–75). Worst in dry spells after the rains start.
- **Scout:** 20 plants in a W pattern; act at ≥ 10 % fresh damage (≥ 20 % after knee height).
- **Manage:** hand-pick egg masses; push-pull (Desmodium + Brachiaria border); biologicals (Bt, Metarhizium); registered insecticide into the whorl, early morning.

## Late Blight (*Phytophthora infestans*): potato, tomato
- **Signs:** dark water-soaked leaf patches with pale edges, white mould under leaves in humid mornings, brown stem lesions, rotting tubers.
- **Risk window:** cool (12–20 °C), wet weather; can destroy a field in 7–10 days.
- **Manage:** certified seed; wider spacing; remove infected plants; protectant fungicide *before* rain; rotate 3 seasons away from potato/tomato.

## Aphids: kale, beans, cabbage
- **Signs:** clusters of small soft insects under leaves, curled or cupped leaves, sticky honeydew, black sooty mould, ants on plants.
- **Manage:** strong water spray; soap or neem solution; conserve ladybirds and hoverflies; registered aphicide only when colonies spread.

## Bean fly (*Ophiomyia* spp.): beans
- **Signs:** wilting seedlings, swollen or cracked stem base, small holes on leaves (adult feeding), maggots in stem.
- **Manage:** earth-up stems; plant early with the rains; treated seed next season; rotate.

## Maize Lethal Necrosis (MLN): maize
- **Signs:** yellow streaks from leaf edges, leaves dry from the margins, "dead heart", poorly filled cobs.
- **Manage:** certified seed of tolerant varieties; rogue infected plants; break the maize cycle with beans or potato for one season.

## Tomato leaf miner (*Tuta absoluta*): tomato
- **Signs:** blotch-shaped mines in leaves, holes in fruit, frass.
- **Manage:** pheromone traps, remove infested leaves, insect-proof nets in greenhouses.

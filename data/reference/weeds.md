# Common weeds and their control (EDUCATIONAL GUIDANCE)

| Weed | Where | Why it matters | Control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Striga (witchweed) | maize, sorghum | Parasitic: attaches to roots, can cut yields by more than half | Push-pull with Desmodium, Striga-tolerant maize, rotation with legumes, hand-pull before flowering |
| Couch grass (*Digitaria scalarum*) | all fields | Spreads by rhizomes, competes for water and nitrogen | Deep ploughing in the dry season, remove rhizomes, mulching |
| Black-jack (*Bidens pilosa*) | maize, beans | Fast-growing, hosts pests | Weed at 2–3 and 5–6 weeks after emergence, before seeding |
| Wandering Jew (*Commelina benghalensis*) | moist plots | Re-roots from stem pieces | Remove and dry fully off the plot; do not leave pieces |
| Nutgrass (*Cyperus rotundus*) | irrigated plots | Tubers survive hoeing | Repeated shallow weeding to exhaust tubers; dense crop cover |

**Rule of thumb:** keep maize and beans weed-free for the first **6 weeks**. That's when competition costs the most yield.

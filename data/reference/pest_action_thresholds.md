# Pest & disease action thresholds — Dagoretti South extension desk (desk guidance, synthetic)

## Officer targets
- **Act only on verified alerts** within the action radius: Fall Armyworm 5 km, Late Blight 3 km, aphids 2 km.
- **Outbreaks older than 30 days** no longer trigger a SPRAY.

> Written for this demo from general public extension practice. Not an official MoA document.
> An officer must confirm product choice and dose against current PCPB-registered labels.

| Pest / disease | Action radius | Susceptible stages | Action when verified nearby |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall Armyworm (maize) | 5 km | emergence to tasseling (days 0–75) | Scout 20 plants in a W pattern; if ≥ 10 % show fresh window-pane damage, spray a registered product into the whorl, early morning. |
| Late Blight (potato, tomato) | 3 km | vegetative to tuber bulking / fruiting | Protective fungicide before rain; remove infected leaves; do not irrigate overhead. |
| Aphids (kale, beans) | 2 km | all leafy stages | Soap-water or registered aphicide; check undersides of leaves. |
| Bean fly (beans) | 3 km | emergence and early vegetative | Earth up stems; seed dressing next season. |

Rule: never spray when the spray-safety index is UNSAFE (rain likely within 24 h); hold and re-check.

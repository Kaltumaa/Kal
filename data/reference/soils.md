# Soils of Nairobi and surroundings (desk guidance, synthetic)

## Officer targets
- **pH:** 5.8–7.0 for maize, beans and vegetables; 5.0–6.5 for potato. Below 5.5, advise lime only after a soil test.
- **Soil test:** every 2–3 seasons per plot; record the result on the household profile.
- **Black cotton soil:** raised beds before the rains; advise against working it when wet.
- **Slopes over 5 %:** contour planting and grass strips before planting.

| Soil | Texture & behaviour | Good for | Care |
|---|---|---|---|
| Red volcanic loam (nitisol) | Deep, well-drained, holds nutrients | Maize, beans, potato, kale, coffee | Add manure; control erosion on slopes |
| Black cotton soil (vertisol) | Heavy clay, cracks when dry, waterlogs when wet | Sorghum, cotton, some vegetables on raised beds | Raised beds; avoid working when wet; add organic matter |
| Sandy loam | Light, drains fast, low nutrients | Sweet potato, groundnut, carrots | Mulch; frequent small manure doses |
| Clay loam | Holds water, fertile | Kale, cabbage, tomato | Ensure drainage; don't compact |

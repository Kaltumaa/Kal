# Spray-safety guidance (SYNTHETIC GUIDANCE)

Spray-safety index = 100 − min(70, 7 × rain_24h) − min(25, 0.5 × (rain_72h − rain_24h) × (1 + runoff)) − storm penalty
(storm penalty: LOW 0, MODERATE 15, HIGH 40)

* SAFE (≥ 70): spray early morning or late afternoon.
* CAUTION (40–69): spray only if no rain is forecast for the next 6 hours; use a sticker.
* UNSAFE (< 40): do not spray — product will wash off and run into water sources.
* Flash-washout: ≥ 50 mm in 72 h or HIGH storm risk → HOLD all field operations.
